Option buyers dream of 10x. Option sellers love their high win rates. Both can go broke.

Buyers pay a premium for the chance of a big move. Their risk is limited to what they paid, but time works against them and many trades lose small.

Sellers collect premium and are right more often. But their gain is capped at the premium, and an unhedged position can lose many times that on one big move.

Neither side is 'better'. The edge comes from how options are priced, how big you trade and how you control risk.
