Your strategy won 19 of its last 30 trades. Is it good?

Maybe. Or maybe it's a coin flip having a lucky month.

If a strategy has zero edge (a true 50% win rate), there's still an 18% chance it wins 18 or more of 30 trades. That's a 60%+ win rate from pure luck, roughly 1 time in 5.

Small samples are noisy. Before trusting any system, backtest or live, you need enough trades for the edge to stand out from the noise. Think hundreds, across different market conditions.

Save this before you judge your next strategy on one good month.
