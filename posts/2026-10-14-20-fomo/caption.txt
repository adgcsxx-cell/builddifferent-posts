The market runs without you. Your feed is full of profit screenshots. You jump in.

That's FOMO, and it tends to buy near the top.

When you chase a move:
→ You enter late, with the stop far away or missing
→ You take a setup your rules never approved
→ You're the last buyer before the pullback

A missed trade costs ₹0. A chased trade can cost a lot. There's always another setup tomorrow.

Save this for the next time a move makes you feel left behind.
