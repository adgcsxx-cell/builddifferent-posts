Strategy A wins 80% of its trades. Strategy B wins only 35%. Which one makes money?

B does.
→ A: wins ₹500 on average, loses ₹2,500. Expectancy: −₹100 per trade.
→ B: wins ₹3,000 on average, loses ₹1,000. Expectancy: +₹400 per trade.

Expectancy = (win % × average win) − (loss % × average loss). It tells you what one trade is worth, on average, over many trades.

A high win rate feels great, but feelings don't pay. Know your expectancy after costs.
