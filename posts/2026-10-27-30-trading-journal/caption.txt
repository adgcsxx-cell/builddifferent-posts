The fastest way to become a better trader isn't a new indicator. It's a notebook.

Log every trade: setup, planned entry, stop and target, size, exit reason, result in rupees and in R, whether you followed your rules, and how you felt.

After 50–100 trades, the journal starts talking. You'll see which setups really pay, when you trade badly, and how much breaking your own rules is costing you.

A backtest tests your strategy. A journal tests you.
