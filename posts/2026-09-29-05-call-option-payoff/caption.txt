Options sound complicated. The core idea isn't.

A call option gives you the right, not the obligation, to buy at a fixed price (the strike) before expiry. You pay a premium for that right.

In the example (strike 100, premium 5):
→ Below 100 at expiry: the option expires worthless. You lose 5, never more.
→ At 105: you break even.
→ Above 105: profit grows with the price.

The catch is time. Every day, part of that premium melts away. That's the next lesson in this series.
