Two traders take the exact same trades. Both win 50% of the time.

One follows the plan: wins average ₹2,000, losses ₹1,000. That's +₹500 per trade on average.

The other books profits early and gives losers "one more day": wins shrink to ₹1,000, losses grow to ₹1,500. That's −₹250 per trade.

Same entries. The only difference is when they exit.

This is the disposition effect: closing a winner feels good, closing a loser feels like admitting a mistake. Rules set before the trade are how you beat it.

Which one do you struggle with more: cutting winners or holding losers?
