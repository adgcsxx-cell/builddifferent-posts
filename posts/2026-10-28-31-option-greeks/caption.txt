Why did your call lose money when the market went up? The Greeks can tell you.

→ Delta: how much the option moves for a 1-point move in the underlying
→ Gamma: how fast delta changes (biggest near the strike and near expiry)
→ Theta: the value lost each day to time decay
→ Vega: the change in price for a 1-point change in implied volatility

They all act at the same time. A small price gain can be wiped out by theta or a drop in IV.
