"My strategy wins 70% of the time." Great, but how much do you win versus lose?

A win rate only means something next to the payoff:
→ Win the same as you lose (1:1): you need 50% winners to break even.
→ Win twice what you lose (2:1): just 33%.
→ Win three times (3:1): only 25%.

The formula: break-even win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + R), where R is your average win divided by your average loss. Add costs and you need a little more.

Know both numbers for your own trading. Which one do you track today?
