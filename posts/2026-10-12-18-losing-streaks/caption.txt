Five losses in a row doesn't mean your strategy is broken. It usually means you've taken enough trades.

Over 100 trades, the chance of seeing at least 5 losses in a row:
→ 40% win rate: 98%
→ 50% win rate: 81%
→ 60% win rate: 46%

Even good systems go through ugly stretches. Traders who don't know this abandon good strategies at exactly the wrong time.

Work out what's normal for your system before the streak arrives. Then size so you can survive it.
