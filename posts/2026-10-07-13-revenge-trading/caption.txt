You take a loss. It stings. Now you want it back, today.

So you size up, take a setup you'd normally skip, and lose again, bigger this time. That's the revenge loop, and it has emptied more trading accounts than bad strategies ever have.

The fix isn't willpower. It's rules decided in advance:
→ A daily loss limit that ends your day
→ A pause after 2–3 losses in a row
→ Size never goes up to recover a loss

The market will be open tomorrow. Make sure your account is too.
