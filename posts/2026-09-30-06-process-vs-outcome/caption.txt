The market pays you randomly in the short run. That's why results are a terrible teacher.

There are four kinds of trades:
✅ Good process, good outcome: deserved win
☑️ Good process, bad outcome: bad luck
⚠️ Bad process, good outcome: dumb luck
❌ Bad process, bad outcome: deserved loss

The dangerous one is dumb luck. You broke your rules, got paid, and now your brain wants to do it again.

Grade your trades on process. Over enough trades, the P&L follows.
