Where do you put your stop-loss? If the answer is "wherever it starts to hurt", read on.

A stop based on pain ("I'll exit at −₹5,000") sits at a random spot the market doesn't care about. It gets hit by normal noise, and then price carries on in your direction without you.

A logical stop sits where your trade idea is proven wrong: for example, just below a floor that price has respected. If that level breaks, the reason for the trade is gone.

Pick the logical stop first. Then size your position so that stop equals the risk you planned.
