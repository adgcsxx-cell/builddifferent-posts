A 50% loss needs a 100% gain to recover. That's not opinion, it's arithmetic.

When you lose, the next gain is calculated on a smaller base. So the deeper the hole, the steeper the climb:
→ −10% needs +11%
→ −30% needs +43%
→ −50% needs +100%
→ −70% needs +233%

This is why professional traders obsess over the downside first. Small, controlled losses are easy to repair. One big loss can undo years of good work.

Save this for the next time you're tempted to skip a stop-loss.
