You hear "Nifty is up 1%" every day. But what is it actually measuring?

The Nifty 50 is a basket of 50 large companies on the NSE, turned into one number. It's weighted by free-float market capitalisation, so the biggest companies (counting only shares available to trade) have the most influence.

A few things most beginners don't know:
→ The members are reviewed twice a year, so the basket changes
→ You can't buy the index itself; you use index funds, ETFs, futures or options
→ The Sensex is the BSE's version, with 30 companies
