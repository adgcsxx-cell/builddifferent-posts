How much you risk per trade matters more than how you pick trades.

Take the same streak of 10 losing trades:
→ Risking 1% per trade: down about 10%
→ Risking 2%: down about 18%
→ Risking 5%: down about 40%

To lose 20% of your account you'd need 23 losses in a row at 1% risk, but only 5 at 5% risk.

Streaks happen to every trader and every system. Your position size decides whether a streak is a bad week or the end of your account.
