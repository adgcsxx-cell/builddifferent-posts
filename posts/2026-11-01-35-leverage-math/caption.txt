Leverage feels like free extra buying power. The math says otherwise.

Loss on your margin = price move × leverage.

A 4% move against you costs:
→ 1x: 4% of your margin
→ 2x: 8%
→ 5x: 20%
→ 10x: 40%
→ 20x: 80%

And the move that wipes out your margin completely is 100% ÷ leverage. At 10x that's just a 10% move. At 20x, 5%.

Leverage doesn't improve a setup. It only makes every result bigger, including the losses, and it shrinks the room your trade has to breathe.

Work out your size from your stop and your risk per trade, not from how much leverage the broker allows.
