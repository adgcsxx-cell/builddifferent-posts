Five wins in a row feels like proof. It usually isn't.

Take a trader with no edge at all: a 50% win rate, like a coin flip. Over 100 trades, the chance of at least one 5-win streak is about 81%. A 6-win streak: about 55%. Even over just 20 trades, a 5-win streak shows up about 25% of the time.

The danger isn't the streak. It's what we do next: we feel skilled, size up, and a perfectly normal losing run lands on the biggest positions.

Keep your risk per trade the same after wins, and judge your system on a large sample.
