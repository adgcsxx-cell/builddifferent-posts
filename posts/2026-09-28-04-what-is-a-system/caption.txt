Most traders don't have a system. They have a feeling.

A real trading system answers four questions in advance:
1. Setup: what am I trading, and when?
2. Entry: what exactly gets me in?
3. Exit: where do I accept I'm wrong, and where do I take profit?
4. Size: how much am I risking?

Write all four down before the market opens. If two people could read your rules and take different trades, the rules aren't finished.
