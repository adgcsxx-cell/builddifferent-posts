Your backtest says the worst drawdown was 9R. Should you plan for 9R?

We took 100 trades (45 wins of +1.5R, 55 losses of −1R) and shuffled their order 20,000 times. Every version ends with exactly the same profit: +12.5R.

But the worst drawdown ranged from 3.5R to 24R. One order in 20 hit 14.5R or worse.

The order of wins and losses is luck, and your backtest shows you just one order. Size for the bad-luck version, not the lucky one.

(R = amount risked per trade. Simulation, not real trades.)
