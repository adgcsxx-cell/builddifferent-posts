Profit factor is one of the simplest backtest numbers: gross profit ÷ gross loss.

Example: 100 trades, 45 winners averaging ₹1,500 and 55 losers averaging ₹1,000.
→ Gross profit: ₹67,500
→ Gross loss: ₹55,000
→ Profit factor: 1.23

Above 1, the system makes money. Below 1, it loses.

Now add ₹100 of costs to every trade. Winners shrink to ₹1,400, losers grow to ₹1,100, and the profit factor drops to 1.04. Almost nothing left.

So always read it after costs, with enough trades, and next to the drawdown.
