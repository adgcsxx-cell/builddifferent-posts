Want to know how fast something doubles? Divide 72 by the yearly rate.

At 6% a year: 72 ÷ 6 = 12 years. The exact answer is 11.9 years. At 8%: about 9 years. At 12%: about 6.

It works for anything that compounds, including the things working against you. At 6% inflation, prices double in about 12 years, so ₹1,00,000 then buys what about ₹49,700 buys today.

Use it as a quick sense check: on inflation, on fees, and on any return that sounds too good to be true.

(Rates here are assumptions for the math, not forecasts or recommendations.)
