Want to grow faster? Just risk more per trade, right?

We simulated one strategy (45% win rate, winners 1.5× the size of losers) for 200 trades, 20,000 times, at different risk levels. Typical worst drawdown:
→ 0.5% risk: ~6%
→ 1% risk: ~12%
→ 2% risk: ~22%
→ 5% risk: ~49%

In the unlucky 1-in-20 runs, 5% risk meant a drawdown of 73%.

Bigger size only speeds up growth if you survive the drawdowns. Pick your risk per trade from the drawdown you can actually live with, emotionally and financially.

(Simulation, not real results.)
