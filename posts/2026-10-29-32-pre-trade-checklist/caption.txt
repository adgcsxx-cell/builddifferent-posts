Airline pilots have thousands of hours of experience, and they still run a checklist before every take-off. Under pressure, anyone skips steps.

Trading is the same. The worst trades usually aren't bad ideas; they're good ideas executed badly: no stop, the wrong size, a trade right before a big announcement, or a trade taken out of boredom.

Use the 6-point checklist on the slides before every order. If any answer is 'no', the trade doesn't happen.

Save it, and keep it next to your screen.
