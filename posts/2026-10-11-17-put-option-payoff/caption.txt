If a call is a bet on a rise, a put is the mirror image.

A put option gives you the right (not the obligation) to sell at a fixed price, the strike, before expiry. You pay a premium for it.

Example (strike 100, premium 5):
→ Above 100 at expiry: it expires worthless. You lose 5, never more.
→ At 95: break-even.
→ Below 95: profit grows as the price falls.

Many investors use puts like insurance: a small, known cost to limit the damage from a big fall.
