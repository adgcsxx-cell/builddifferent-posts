Your strategy makes ₹250 per trade on average. Costs take ₹200. You're left with ₹50.

In a backtest without costs, that looks like a great system. In real life, 80% of the edge is gone before it reaches your account.

Brokerage, exchange charges, taxes and slippage are small on one trade. Over hundreds of trades, they're often the difference between a strategy that works and one that doesn't. The more often you trade, the more they matter.

Always backtest with realistic costs, and check your actual contract notes.
