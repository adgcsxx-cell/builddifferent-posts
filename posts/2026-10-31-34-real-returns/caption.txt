₹1,00,000 growing at 7% a year becomes about ₹3,86,968 in 20 years. Looks great.

Now add 6% inflation. In today's prices, that money buys only what about ₹1,20,659 buys now.

The real return is (1.07 ÷ 1.06) − 1 = 0.94% a year. Not 7%. Not even 1%.

That's why the headline number can mislead. What matters is the gap between your return and inflation, after costs and taxes.

The rates here are assumptions for the math, not a forecast or a recommendation.
